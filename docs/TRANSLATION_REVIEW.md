## Requirement texts

The requirement texts shown in the application are translations of the project's Spanish reference document. They are **not verbatim quotes** of NASA-STD-3001 (published in English). Verify wording against the standard before any real use.

## Known gaps in the English version

- The symptom analyzer and the floating Flight Surgeon assistant still accept Spanish input only.
- The safety filter detects definitive-diagnosis wording in Spanish only.
- Typos inherited from the PDF extraction in `docs/fuentes/` were translated without correction (for example "msv/día" became "msv/day").
- URL paths remain in Spanish (`/tripulacion`, `/indicador`, ...) by design.
