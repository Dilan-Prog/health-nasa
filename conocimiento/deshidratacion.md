## Signs to watch
- Hydration in caution or critical according to the project's thresholds (the reference guide sets hydration below 2.0 L/day as an amber condition).
- As supporting signs, altered pulse, temperature or blood pressure in the same window.

## What to check
- The fluid intake logged for the day and whether any entry is missing.
- The action the guide gives for that condition: notify the crew member and the Flight Surgeon and adjust the daily ration plan.
- Compare against the crew member's own baseline.

## When to escalate
- The system sends the case to the ground when its severity is critical (project rule, not a standard).
- Additional clinical escalation criteria: Pending verifiable source.

## Limits of what can be stated
- Only a possible dehydration (hypothesis): the system does not measure body water or kidney function.
- That pulse, temperature and pressure accompany the hypothesis is not supported by the reference documents: Pending verifiable source.
- Hydration is logged once a day, so the pattern window is wide (project assumption).
